Inscription: SP-06b
Period:	circa 13th century CE
Location: Fixed near the right pilaster, besides the mihrab
Language:	Prakrit
Script:	Nagari

Text:

..nn..||3||tāḍako.taka.yālevalayānemuktāla.vaśāgonavatmaratnavalāsaka. pū..........

Gist: Fragmentary and defaced. Seems to be a part of a bogger slab, which probably

	number 3. Palaeographically datable to 13th century CE.
