Inscription No.: PMR_SP01
Period: circa 13th century CE
Location: Southern Colonnade (Recovered from the wall of the mihrab in 1903-04)
Dynasty: Paramāra
King: Arjunavarman
Language: Sanskrit and Prakrit
Script: Nagari
Gist: Records the eulogy of the Paramāra Kings and contains the first two acts of a 
	drama entitled Pārijātamañjarī-nāṭikā or Vijayaśrī, composed by Madana, the
	preceptor (rājaguru) of the King Arjunavarman. 
Published reference: Annual Report on Indian Epigraphy, No.B.112 of 1950-51.
	Epigraphia Indica Vol.VIII, pp.96 ff.
