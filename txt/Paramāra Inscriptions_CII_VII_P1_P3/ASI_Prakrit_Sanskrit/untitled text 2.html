No. 3; PLATE III
THE SECOND PLATE OF AHMEDABAD GRANT OF SĪYAKA sāmānyaṃ caitat puṇya phalaṃ vubuddhvāsmad vaṃśajair anyair api bhāvi bhoktṛbhir asmat pradatta dharmma dāyoya- m anugamantavyappāvyaḥ pālamīnīyaś aś ca | uktaṃ ca bhagavatā vyāsena | vabahubhir vvasudhā bhuktā rājabhiḥ sagarādibhiḥ | yasya yasya yadā bhūmis tsya tasya tadā phalaṃlam ||1|| yānīha dattāni purā narenddrair ddanāni dharmmārtha yaśaṣ kas karāṇi | nimmārmma - lyavāntaḥ pratimāni tāni ko nāma sādhuṣpudhuḥ punar ādadīta ||2|| asmat kulakramam udāram udāharadbhir anyai- śca dāmanam idam abhyanumodanīyaṃyam | lakṣmyās taḍit salila vudvubudbuda cacaṃcalāyāḥ dānanaṃ phalaṃ parayaśa- ṣpaḥ paripālanaṃ ca ||3|| sarvvān etān bhāvinaṣ pāḥ pārthivendrādbhūn bhūyo bhūyo yācecate rāma bhadraḥ | sāmānyo- yayaṃ dharmma setur nṛpāṇāṃ | kāle kāle pālanīyo bhavadbhiḥ ||4|| iti kamala dalāmvumbu uvibindu lolāṃ śri- yam anucintya manuṣya jīvitataṃ ca | sakalamidam udāhṛtataṃ ca vubuddhvā na hi puruṣaiṣpaṣaiḥ para kīrtta- yo vilopyāḥ ||5|| iti | saṃ 1026 āśvina vadi 15 | svayam ājñā | dāpakaś cātra kanhapaikaḥ | śrīsīyakasya sva hastoyaṃyam
