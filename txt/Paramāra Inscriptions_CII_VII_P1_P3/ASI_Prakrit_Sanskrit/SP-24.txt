Inscription No: SP-24
Period: circa 13th century CE
Location: Defaced, built into the floor
Language: Prakrit
Script: Nagari

Text: 

..i| sayavāraṃ āvattājeṇae āṇateṇa. ||232||...navā .....sakalavaku...dasadanayadānaḥ sacāṇā.

Gist: Fragmentary and defaced. Seems to be a part of a bigger slab, which probably
contained some literary work. Mentions verse number 232. Palaeographically
datable to 13th century CE.

Published Reference: Annual Report on Indian Epigraphy, No. B. 51 of 1988 - 89.
