Period: VS 1344 
Location: Ajaygaḍh
King: BHojavarman
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose
Gist: Records that  Suhaḍadēva caused to be made (carved) the images of several gods.
Published reference: Trivedi (1989), no. 193.


Text{1}

1 || auṃ namaḥ śrī kedārāya{2} || kāyasthavāstavyavaṃśe pratolībhiḥ saha jayapuradurggasya ṭhavakuraśrīvīdana{3}tatputra
  śrīvāśe tasyātmajaṭhakkuraśrī aṣau sutasuhaḍadevenesya{4}mūrtti-śrīkedāra-{5}pārvvatīvṛṣabha-kṛṣṇā{6}- amvi(mbi)kāḥ
  tārā-tripurā-kāmākṣā-durggādevī-harasiddhi-aindrī-cāmuṇḍā-kālikā-īśvara{7}-pārvvatīvṛṣabha etāḥ pratimā-
  kāritāḥ || saṃvat 1344 samaye || śubhama[stu] ||
  
  --------------------------------------
  
{1} From the impression, which is No. B-192/69-70 of A. R. of Ind. Ep.
{2} The sign of mātrā of the first akṣara of the name is detached from it. 
{3} This and the names that follow are all without case endings, which can easily be supplied.
{4} The consonants of this and the preceding letter are conjunct; but I am unable to make them out from 
	the impression. The reading of both these letters is uncertain.
{5} In the transcript, the names are separated by me with horizontal strokes for facility of reading.
{6} The mātrā is denoted by a curve above.
{7} A flourish above the first of these letters denoting the mātrā is to be seen.
