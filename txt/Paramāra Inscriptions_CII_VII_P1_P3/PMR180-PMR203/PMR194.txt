PennGlobal: PMR194
Period: VS 1366 śrāvaṇa śudi 10
Location: Pannā
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose 
Gist: Inscription record falls into two parts, Part A and B. 
	It records the installation of a group of images by Suhaḍadēva, 
	the son of Ashau and the grandson of Vāśē, born in the Vāstavya Kāyastha family
Published reference: Trivedi (1989), no. 194.


Text{1}

Part A

1. śrīḥ[|*] paṃ.{2} | suhaḍadevasya patnī devalladevībhire[-]-{3}
2. ......{4}pratimāḥ...{5}
3. ..........rudradevena sva....

Part B

1. siddham{6} [|*] siddhiḥ || saṃ.1366 śrāvaṇa su(śu)di 10 gurau kāsya(śya)pānvayakāyasthavāsthavāstavyavaṃśe kā-
2. laṃjarajayapurayo [ḥ] pratolikānvita....{7} ṭhakuraśrīvāse(śe)pautreṇa ṭhakuraaṣauputreṇa paṃ[ca]-
3. rudradeve[sa(śa)?]sya ........{8} samastapratimāḥ{9} kāritāḥ || 

----------------------------------------------------------------

{1} From an impression which is No. C-1996-97 of A. R., Ind. Ep., 1963-64.
{2} The reading of this akṣara is absolutely certain. Whether the vertical after it is a part of a letter is 
	not known. 
{3} The letter lost here is to be restored as tā; Read devyā and the preceding word as patnyā.
{4} Six syllables are lost here. They should be taken as showing the names of some deities.
{5} The lost letters may have been kāritāḥ followed by a punctuation mark. 
{6} Denoted by a symbol. 
{7} Four akṣaras are indistinct here.
{8} Five akṣaras are lost here, leaving only traces.
{9} Better read the first of these words separately, both in the nominative plural.
