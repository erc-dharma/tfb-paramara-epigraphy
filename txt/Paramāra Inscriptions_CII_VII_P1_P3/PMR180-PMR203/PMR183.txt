PennGlobal: PMR183
Period: VS 1243 pauṣa śudi 5
Location: Hoshangabad
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
King: Mahākumāra Hariśchandra
Text: Prose 
Gist: To record the death of a Hero
Published reference: Trivedi (1989), no. 183.


Text{1}

1. saṃvat 12[4]3{2} paukha(ṣa) śu-
2. di 5 gurau mahāku-
3. māraśrīhariścaṃdra-
4. devarājye vijayasīṃ(siṃ)ha-{3}
5. devasutavīmba sīṃ(miṃ)ha-
6. ha------tālādevaiḥ{4}
7. yu[dhī]tya(yudhvā) devai............{5}

------------------------------------------------
{1} From the original stone.
{2} The unit figure is mutilated but its reading is certain. As for the decimal figure, the curve at its 
	lowest extremity is damaged and partly disappeared, making it appear also as 5. But my close and 
	minute examination shows it to be 4, as taken here, which is also consistent with the period assigned
	to Hariśchandra, mentioned in the inscription.
{3} The reading of both the names is uncertain.
{4} Some three akṣaras are lost at the beginning of this line, and in the next one, the letters are partly
	imbedded below.
{5} The inscription appears to be incomplete and it is not known as to how many lines are below the surface.
