Location: Rāsin
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose and Metrical in a verse
Metres: Anuṣṭhubh 
Gist: Epigraph is a pilgrm's record, and it registers the name of one Jaipāla the son of Surapāla, 
	who visited the place to pay homage to the diety enshrined in the temple where it is engrained.
