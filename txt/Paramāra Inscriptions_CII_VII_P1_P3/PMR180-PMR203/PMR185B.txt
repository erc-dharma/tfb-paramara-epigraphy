PennGlobal: PMR185B
Period: Lost
Location: Dhār
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Metrical
Metres: All in Anuṣṭhubh 
Gist: Begins by paying homage to Sarasvatī; the inscription eulogises the deity as fulfilling all desires.
Published reference: Trivedi (1989), no. 185B.

B

Text{1}

1. [oṃ namaḥ] sarasvatyai || tvayi kalpalatā ......
2. ......bhinna bhāvā{2} jayati paraṃ bhāratī......
3. ......[sa]kalā dharmmāḥ sarasvatyekasaṃśrayāḥ || a......
4. ......vāmiti [dhva]ni{3}mayyapi || bhāratī bhā[ti]....
5. dbhavo bhavatu cedbhavatī mayā niyā......
6. ratirbhavati kasya na yogino{4} ......
7. .....[a]ṅkacchandasāṃ bhedā mūrttiṃ [da?]tte sarasva[tyāḥ].....
8. .....satkaviḥ sa kimucyatāṃ | ni......
9. .....[a]nyonyo{5} (?). .rnasyānmahākaviḥ......
10. .....tairdharitrī nibhṛtā bhṛtā......
11. .....so'nugrahaḥ ravalu giraḥ{6} ......
12. .....caturvvarggāya vālmīkivyā[sādayaḥ].....
13. .....{7}ghoṭayannaṃdinīṃ vīreśa[ḥ].....
14. .....[ni]vartya ca raṇe{8}dhenuḥ.....
15. .....[dabhu]dbhūpatidarśana[m]

{1} From the original and also from the facsimile  accompanying Lele's article. 
{2} Two distinct letters, probably to be read as vaśī, are partly visible before this word.
{3} The letter in brackets is partly rubbed out and the reading is therefore uncertain.
{4} The mātrā of short i is visible after this akṣara.
{5} The first three akṣaras are indistinct and the reading is not certain. It is adopted here from Lele's
	reading. 
{6} The akṣaras in this line read like a foot of Vasantatilakā.
{7} One letter in the beginning of this line is indistinct. It may have been sa.
{8} To be split up as done here and not be taken as one word as taken by Lele, as caraṇē.
