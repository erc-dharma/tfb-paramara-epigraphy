PennGlobal: PMR180
Period: VS 1120 kārttika vadi 13
Location: Wall of Amarēśvara temple  at Māndhāta
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose and Metrical in 71 verses
Metres: Verse 1-16 Mandākrāntā; vv.62, 64-71 Anuṣṭubh; v.63 Śārdūlavikrīḍita
Gist: The theme of the stōtra is praise to Śiva. 
	Provides the 12 principal names of Śiva. 
	Also gives the names of a few Saivite teachers and that in the city of Bhōja, 
	living in the Sōmēśvaradēva monastery and hailing from Nandiya was the Pāśupta teacher Bhaṭṭāraka, 
	the illustrious Bhavavālmīka, whose disciple was Bhaṭṭāraka, the illustrious Bhāva-samudra.
Published reference: Trivedi (1989), no. 180.
