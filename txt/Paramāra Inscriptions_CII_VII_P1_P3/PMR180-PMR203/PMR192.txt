PennGlobal: PMR192
Period: 1335 caitra śudi 13
Location: Ajayagaḍh
King: Vīravarman
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose and Metrical in 2 verses
Metres: Verse 1 Vasantatilaka; v.2 Anuṣṭhubh
Gist: Registers the installation of an image of Śāntinatha, the sixteen of the Jaina pontiffs, 
	by the sādhu Sōḍhala, the son of sādhu Sīḍhala and Dēvakī residing 
	at the Jayapura-durgga and belonging to the Grahapati family.
Published reference: Trivedi (1989), no. 192.


Text{1}

1. siddham{2} | oṃ namo vītarāgāya || śrīmadvīravarmmadevavijayarājye saṃvata(t) 1335 caitra su(śu)di 13 some 
   jayapura[du]rggo{3}ga(gra)hapatiśrī-
2. sādhuśrīmāsala{4}tasya suta sādhu soḍhala | dāmodara | gaṃgādhara | jaso(yaśo)dhara nityaṃ [praṇamanti]{5} [|*]
3. cetaḥ paropakṛtikarmmaṭhamanya[to]ṣavyāhārasāracikurā{6}madhurā ca vāṇī | gātraṃ sadā jinapadapraṇatipraṇītapāvitrya-
   pātramamalaṃ{7}ca vi-˘
4. tasya ||1|| sīṭasādhu pitā ja(ya)sya devakī jananī tathā | sādhunā soṭalenāyaṃ śāntinātha[ḥ*] pratiṣṭhitaḥ{8} ||
   tasya suta nānadeva nityaṃ praṇamanti(ti) ||{9}
   
----------------------------------------------------------

{1} From a photograph.
{2} Expressed by a symbol.
{3} The akṣaram in brackets shows an unnecessary curve above.
{4} This and the other proper names below are all without case ending but separated by a daṇḍa.
{5} The bracketed letters are all lost. Rather read ete nityaṃ . . . . .
{6} The akṣara in brackets is formed as va by a wrong stroke of the chisel, as some others also, which
	are not noted separately.
{7} The same as above, in the form of trya and tra.
{8} The correct word required here is pratiṣṭhāpitaḥ, which would not suit the metre.
{9} I am not certain about the closing sign in the end.
