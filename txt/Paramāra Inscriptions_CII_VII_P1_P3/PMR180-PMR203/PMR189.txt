PennGlobal: PMR189
Period: VS 1356 jyeṣṭha vadi 5
Location: Varmān
King: Vikramasimha
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose 
Gist: The object is not clear, but it appears to record some donations made to the temple or 
	some repairs made to it by Latatadēva, the wife of Chīcharī or Chāchiga.
Published reference: Trivedi (1989), no. 189.


Text{1}

1. svasti{2} [|*] saṃvat 1356 varṣe yeṣṭa(jyeṣṭha) vadi 5 so-
2. me vra(bra)hmāṇamahāsthāne mahārā-
3. jakulaśrīvikramasī(siṃ)hakalyā-
4. ṇavijayarāje(jye) padyā-{3} rāja. cī-
5. caibhājā{4}lalatada[o]vaśrīvra(bra)hmā-
6. ṇasvāmikarā[ya ?]{5} taṃ{6}........

------------------------------------------

{1} From the original and inked estampages.
{2} Expressed by a symbol which is partly visible. 
{3} This and the following abbreviations I am unable to make out. The reading of this letter too is
	doubtful and the horizontal stroke of the mātrā is joined to the letter, as in the case of dhā.
{4} It is impossible to say whether Chīchaī was a royal personage or a private individual. It is equally
	impossible to say whether the word bhājā has to be restored to bhāryā; in that case, however, the 
	name that follows has to be restored to Lalatadēvī, i.e., Lalitādēvī.
{5} The upper part of the loop of the subscript of svā is faintly visible. 
{6} This portion is corrupt. Before the last akṣara, appears a sign resembling a crescent with that of 
	anusvāra above; and the inscription is incomplete.
