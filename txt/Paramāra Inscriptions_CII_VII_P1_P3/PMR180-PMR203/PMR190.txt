PennGlobal: PMR190
Period: VS 1293, phālguṇa śudi 9
Location: Chhatarpur
King: Madanavarman
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose and Metrical in 2 verses
Metres: Anuṣṭhubh  
Gist: Records the installation of the statue of Jinōttama (Śāntinatha) by Lakshimīdhara, 
	the son of Hariśchandra
Published reference: Trivedi (1989), no. 190.

Text{1}

1. siddham{2} | golāpuūrvvānvaye sādhuḥ svayaṃbhūrdharmmavatsalaḥ | tatsutau(taḥ) svāmināmā ca devasvāmiguṇānvitaḥ 
   ||1|| deva[svā]mi{3}-
2. sutau śreṣṭhau su(śu)bhacaṃdrodayacaṃdrakaḥ{4} | kāritaṃ ca jagannāthaṃ{5} śāntinātho jinottamaḥ ||2|| dharmmāsevi - -{6}
3. ṇa tathā du[vā]nvaye{7} sādhu jinacaṃdra tatputrahariścandra tatsutalakṣmīdhara{8} śrīsā(śā)ntināthaṃ praṇamati(nti) sadā |
4. lakṣmīdharasya dharmmasaṃdhije{9} śrīmanmadanavarmmadevarājye samva(saṃva)t 1203 phā{10} sudi 9 some ||

---------------------------------------------------

{1} From a photograph supplied by the Chief Epigraphist.
{2} Expressed by a symbol.
{3} It seems that Dēvaswāmi- was a name and he was a younger brother of Svāmi-. If so, the change of 
	sutau to sutaḥ is not necessary. The construction here is faulty and nothing can be definitely said.
{4} This foot is metrically defective as it has one akṣara more than necessary. Singular is also wrongly
	used instead of dual.
{5} This word is wrongly put in the accusative instead of the nominative, as required by the construction.
{6} Two akṣaras are indistinct here.
{7} The reading of the akṣara in the brackets is not certain and the name of the family cannot be known
	definitely.
{8} All the names in this line are without any case ending, which is nominative.
{9} I am unable to explain the meaning of this expression.
{10} This is phālguna.
