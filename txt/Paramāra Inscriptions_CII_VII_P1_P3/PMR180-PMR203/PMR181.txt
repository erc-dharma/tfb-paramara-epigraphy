PennGlobal: PMR181
Period: Undated
Location: Udaipur
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
King: Udayāditya
Text: Prose and Metrical in 2 verses
Metres: Verses 1-2  Anuṣṭhubh
Gist: Udayāditya named the place Udayapura and also excavated a tank there.
Published reference: Trivedi (1989), no. 181.


Text{1}

A

1. svayaṃbhūraparaḥ śrīmānudayādityabhūpatiḥ |
2. puresva(śva)rasamudrādīnudayopapadānvayadhāt{2} ||1||
3. kimanyairvva(rbba)hubhirvedaiḥ kimanyairva(rba)hubhiḥ sta-
4. vaiḥ | ekacchedādikaṃ vedaṃ śaṃsa sarvvā-
5. rthasiddhidaḥ{3} ||2|| utkīrṇṇāḥ ślokāḥ
6. sūtradhāraśrīmadhusūdanabhrātṛśrī . .{4}
7. devena | maṃgalammahāśrīḥ ||

B

1. ete ślokāḥ{5} samutkīrṇāḥ [sthi]radevena śilpinā[ā]{6}
2. paṃṭitaśrīmahīpālakṛtāḥ sarvvā[rtha]sādhakāḥ ||

---------------------------------------------------

{1} From the original stone. 
{2} The slanting stroke marking this consonant is incised as a mātrā of long ū.
{3} Read daṃ Possibly, also to read ekachatrā in the first foot of this verse. 
{4} Two letters which are lost here can be restored as sthira with the help of B, I. I.
{5} The use of the plural shows that all the verses of No. 181 and of A and B, were engraved by the 
	same person.
{6} The daṇḍa is engraved as a mātrā. The three akṣaras, along with the six at the end of the next 
	line, are now lost and are restored with the help of the illustrations.
