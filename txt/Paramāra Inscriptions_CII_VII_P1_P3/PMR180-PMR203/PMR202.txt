PennGlobal: PMR202
Period: VS 1283
Location: Madhya Pradesh
King: Trailōkyavarman
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose and Metrical in 3 verses
Metres: Verses 1-3 Anuṣṭhubh
Gist: Refers to the victorious reign of king Trailōkyavarman. 
	Records the donation made by him of the village  Varahī in the Vishaya division of Paṭiśato some Brāhmaṇas, 
	from his stay at the fort of Jayapura, which is Ajayagaḍh.
Published reference: Trivedi (1989), no. 202.
