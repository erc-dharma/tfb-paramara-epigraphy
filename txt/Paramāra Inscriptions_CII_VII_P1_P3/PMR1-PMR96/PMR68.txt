PennGlobal: PMR68
Period: VS 1237 Kārttika śudi 11
Location: Hāthal/Hathāra
Dynasty: Paramāra
King: Dhārāvarṣa
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose and metrical in 3 verses.
Metres: Verses 1-7 Anuṣṭhubh
Gist: Records the royal gift, to the effect that Bhaṭṭāraka Vīsala Udagradamaka was permitted to graze his cattle on the pasture grounds, 
	  free of charge, in the village in Sāhilavāḍa.  In addition to the two grants, a piece of land in the village 
	  of Kumbhāranuli up to the boundary and a piece of land that can be tilled with two ploughs in a day were donated.
Published reference: Trivedi (1978), no. 68.
