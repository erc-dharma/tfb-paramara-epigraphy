{1} From Jinavijaya's transcript in his Prācīna Jaina Lekha Saṅgraha, Pt. II, pp. 260-61, No. 426.
{2} That is, saṃvat. The correction in the hundred figure of the year as done here does not appear to be
	necessary.
{3} Read varṣe jyeṣṭha śudi.
{4} Sandhi between Śrī and the following vowel is not performed here for the sake of clarity, as in Śrī-
	Udayāditya, as often noted by us.
{5} Read -rājye. 
{6} An expression like vyāpāre or adhikāre is omitted here.
{7} This akṣara does not stand here for paṃḍita. In the name of the person ṣ appears to have been used 
	for kh, and in the next line the suffix ka is used for the case ending.
{8} Read paṃcakule or kulena. 
{9} Read either ahaṃ ṣe(khe)tākaḥ or mayā ṣe(khe)tākena.
{10} The purpose of the gift and the dimensions of the land appear to have been mentioned here.
{11} A word like pālanīyaṃ and the first half of the customary benedictory verse appear to have lost here.
{12} Read bhūmistasya.
{13} This is the local word for sākṣī.
{14} This akṣara appears to be the abbreviation of brāhmaṇa.
