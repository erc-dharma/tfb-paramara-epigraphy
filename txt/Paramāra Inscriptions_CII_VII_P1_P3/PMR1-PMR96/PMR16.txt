{1} From a set of estampages supplied by the Chief Epigraphist.
{2} R.D. Banerji corrected this word to śrīmān, taking it to be an adjective of yaśovarmmaḥ(rmmā) in 1.8 below, while
	N.P. Chakravarti remarks that we should expect śrīmatyāṃ, as this word goes with dhārāyāṃ (Epi. Ind., Vol.
	XIX,p.71,n.1). I agree with Chakravarti as the adjective is just before the name of the place.
{3} The correct form is paramārānvaye.
{4} After ṭṭa, a letter which was originally engraved has totally disappeared, leaving some traces and a clear anusvāra
	above. The context shows that probably saṃ was put here.
{5} Banerji proposes to cancel the visarga here. But with the visarga this word can as well be taken to be an adjective
    of the word yaśovarmmaḥ(rmmā) 1.8, below. See n.4 above. Va in dhavalita shows redundant additional stroke to
    make it appear as vā.
{6} This letter shows a combination of both the palatal and the dental sibilants. And the horizontal stroke of k
	is also used as a part of the following pa.
{7} Read cedyadhipa in the sense of 'the lord of the Chedis'.
{8} The letters in the brackets are damaged and the reading is not certain.
{9} Read bhoktā.
{10} Read ūdrahādi. The second of these letters is damaged and possibly some other letter was engraved originally
	 and later on corrected.
{11} The daṇḍa here denotes a paranthesis, to connect the preceding word rāṇakena with dattā in 1.17 below. Sandhi
	in śrīamma is not made, as also in same other instances below.
{12} A word like ca or vā is required here.
{13} The daṇḍa is redundant.
{14} The mātrā is clear with some signs of rubbing around.
{15} The sign of visarga appears to have been inserted subsequently.
{16} R.D.B. : cacchāī.
{17} Above the first three of these letters there are redundant chisel strokes. The word kakaḍa appears to have been
	 put here in the sense of kaṅkaṭa.
{18} The editor of the inscription translated this expression as'Vakāigala and others of the town'; but Barnett has 
	 pointed out that nagara is a regular term in the south for 'commercial community'. See D.H.N.I., Vol.II, p.
	 864, n.2. Also see p.72, n.1 of Ep. Ind., op.cit., where it is remarked that Yaśovarman was a feudatory of 
	 Bhoja. In this respect, also see Ep. Ind., Vol.XVIII, p.320, n.1.
{19} A sign for superscript r was originally carved on this letter and subsequently scored as redundant. The mātrā
	 of the preceding ṭṭā is crescent-shaped.
{20} The letters are damaged and the reading is uncertain.
{21} Banerji read Śvetapada and identified it with the northern part of the Nāsik District. But the last akṣara of
	 the name is definitely ṭa as taken here and as also read by D.R. Bhandarkar, to which our attention has been
	 drawn by Prof. V. V. Mirashi in his C.I.I., Vol.IV, p.ixix, n.8, and p.385,n.4. Here the word means Śvetāmbara
	 , which also appears in 1.10, above.
{22} This akṣara is preceded by what looks like a snake. Dēśilaka used in this expression is perhaps in the sense
	 of dēśika, a local officer, or in that of dēśi (also found as dēśi) with probably means the foreman of a guild, for
	 which see C.I.I., Vol.IV,p.195,n.6. Grāmaṭaka is probably used for grāmakūṭa, meaning a nobleman (in
	 the village). Gōkulika may have been the head of cowherds and Chaurika, one in charge of the grazing ground
	 (from char). Śaulkika and daṇḍapāśika are officers in charge of customs and police, respectively, and prātirājyika,
	 was, as the meaning of the word suggests, an officer of the neighbouring kingdom.
{23} Better read ācchindamānaṃ vānumodeta.
{24} The punctuation mark is redundant, as same others below.
{25} Here the visarga is not changed to s, as required by the rule; and this stanza is full of some other errors, e.g.,
	 the root jval is wrongly used in plural and in the Ātmanēpada; the past participle rūḍha is put to denote
	 the present tense; suṣati is used in place of śuṣyati; and its use with the plural sarisaraḥ(for saritsarāṁsi) and
	 chihnānīmāni for chihnānīmāni is again wrong. The whole verse may be rectified as follows:-
	 kiṃ sūryastīvranāpo dahati, śaśikalāḥ pāvako vā pradīptaḥ bhūmau rohenna sasyaṃ na ca janavasatirmeghakāle'lpavṛṣṭi | 
	 goṣu kṣīraṃ praśuṣyejjalamapi ca tathā kṛpavāpīnadīnāṃ yasmmin bhūmipraharttā nivasati viṣaye tatra daurgatyametat ||
{26} Read jāyate, but it would not suit in the metre.
{27} Read rājabhiḥ.
