PennGlobal: PMR35
Period: VS 1191 vaiśāgha śudi 2
Location: Shērgaḍh
Dynasty: Paramāra
King: Naravarman
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text:  Metrical in 5 verses
Metres: Verses 1-3 Śārdūlavikrīḍita; vv.4-5 Anuṣṭubh
Gist: Records the installation of three images of Tirthanṇkaras.
Published reference: Trivedi (1978), no. 35.




Text{1}

1. - - - U U - U[ṇe śrita] U - māhillabhāryāntimā (?),- - - U U - U[ś]sya ti[la]ke sūryāśrame pa[tta]ne | śrīpālo guṇapālakaś ca
   vi[pu-
2. le] [khaṇḍillavā]{2} le kule sūya(ryā)caṃdramasāvivāṃva(ba)ratale prāptau kramān mālave ||1|| śrīpālādiha devapālā tna(tana)yo dāne[na]
   ci[ntā]maṇiḥ śā-
3. [nteḥ]{3} śrīguṇapāla ṭhaku(kku)ra sutād rūpeṇa kāmopamāt [|*] pūnī martha jane [lhu]ka prabhṛva(ta)yaḥ putrāgra(śca)yegrā nava{4}te(taiḥ ) sarvair api
   kośavarddhanata-
4. le ratna trayaḥ(yaṃ){5} kārita(tam) ||2|| varṣai rudraśate(tai)rgataiḥ su(śu)bhatamairekā{6} navatyādhikair vaiśākha(khe) dhavale dvitīya divase devān pratiṣṭhā-
5. pitān | vandante natadevapālatanayā mālhū[ka]sādhānvādayaḥ pūnī [śā]ntisutāś ca nemibharatāḥ{7} śrīśāntisa[tkuṃ]{8}thvarān |
6. |3|| dādisūtradharotpannaḥ(nna)sīlāśrīsūtradhāriṇā [|*] śāntikuṃbhū(thva)ranāma(mā)no jayannu(ntu) ghaṭitā jināḥ ||4|| devapālasu-
7. telhūkaḥ goṣṭhivīsalalalluka(kā)ḥ [|*] māukaḥ haricaṃdrādiḥ [gāgā]sva[su]putra allakaḥ{9} ||5|| saṃvatu(t) 1191 vaisā(śā)ṣa(kha)
   śudi 2 [maṃ]{10} -
8. galadine pratiṣṭhā karāpitā{11} |ḥ||

{1} From facsimile facing p.84 in Ep. Ind., Vol. XXXI.
{2} These four akṣaras are altogether lost in the facsimile and have been adopted from Sircar's reading of
	them.
{3} This akṣara is lost but the preceding sā shows that it was as taken here, as also adopted by Dr. Sircar.
{4} What would metrically be more appropriate is putrā navāgrāśca ye.
{5} This refers to the three images which were constructed and installed there.
{6} The vowel of this akṣara is made long for the metre. It is not known if it may be a mistake for kō(?),
	reading ekonanavat-; and if so, we have to take the last two figures of the year as 89. But nothing can
	be definitely said in this respect.
{7} Sircar read this letter remarked by Sircar, the intended reading is nemibharatādayaḥ.
{8} Sircar read this letter as ku and corrected it to kuṃ. But a faint sign as of the anusvāra is visible on the plate.
{9} The latter half of this verse is metrically defective. The violation of the rules of sandhi, i.e. of not  chang
	ing kaḥ to to kō in two places in this verse, may also be noted.
{10} This letter again is lost and has been restored from the reading of Dr. Sircar. Also see the note in the
	 corresponding portion in the edition above.
{11} Read Kāritā.
