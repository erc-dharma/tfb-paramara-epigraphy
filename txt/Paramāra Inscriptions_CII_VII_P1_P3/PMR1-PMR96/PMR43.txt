PennGlobal: PMR43
Period: Undated
Location: Gyārspur, in Bāsōdā parganā
Dynasty: Paramāra
King: Trailokyavarman
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose
Gist: Consecration of the image of Cāmuṇḍasvāmideva and records the grant of the village 
	  to provide worship of the deity, by mahākumāra Trailokyavarman.
Published reference: Trivedi (1978), no. 43.


Text{1}

1. siddham{2} | svasti || śrī[r*]jayobhyudayaśca || adyeha śrīha[r*][ṣa]purasthitena samastarājā[va]{3} ....
2. tasamastaprakriyāvirājamānamahakumāraśrītrailokyavarmmadevena ....{4}
3. navamyāṃ śrīcāmuṇḍasvāmidevakāritapratiṣṭhāyāṃ{5} pūjānimitte ....
4. [aṣṭāṃ]sa{6}(śa)sahitaṃ devavrā(brā)hmaṇabhuktivarja{7} | grāmoyaṃ śrīcāmuṇḍasvāmi[ne].....{8}

-------------------------------------------------------
{1} From the original stone and inked impression supplied by Dr. Katare.
{2} Expressed by a symbol.
{3} Originally dhi, later on changed to va, Restore the last portion as -līsahi-.
{4} The year and the month are lost here.
{5} The language here is faulty though the sense is clear (K.G.K.).
{6} K.G.K. read bhogyāya, but the first akṣara which begins with a curve is a(misformed), and in view of
	this the second akṣarahas to be read as ṣṭāṃsa(śa), meaning the eighth part (of the revenue?). The
	reading of these akṣaras is due to Dr. Katare.
{7} The anusvāra is clear on the stone but being lightly engraved it could not come in the impression.
{8} Perhaps the lost portion has to be restored by a word like pradattaḥ, sampradattaḥ, or upahṛtaḥ.
