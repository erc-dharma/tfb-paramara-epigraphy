PennGlobal: PMR95
Period: VS 1239 āśvina vadi 10
Location: Bhinmāl
Dynasty: Paramāra
King: Jayatsiṃhadeva
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose
Gist: Records that during the reign of the Mahārāja putra Jayatsiṃha at Śrīmāla, one Aravasāka,
 	  a son  of Paramahidā, presented one dramma, in cash, to Vālākadēva and 
 	  another dramma was offered to the same deity by his wife.
Published reference: Trivedi (1978), no. 95.

Text{1}

1. siddham{2} | saṃ. 1239 [ā]śvina vadi 10 [vu(bu)]dhe
2. adyeha śrī - śrīmāle mahārāja-
3. [putra]śrījayatasīhadevarājye ||{3}
4. guhilo [parama]hidā{4}-suta-tra.{5} arava-
5. sākavahiyaṇa [vālāka]{4} devāya
6. drava(?) dra.{6} 1 tathā[sya] bhāryā mālhaṇade-
7. [vī]kṛta dra. 1 [|*] ye ko'pi ya(ca) [ati]bhava-
8. ti teṣāṃ prati draṃ. vi 1 labhyā(bhyaḥ |) yaḥ ko-
9. pi [ccha]{7}trapālo bhavati tena varṣā[nu]-
10. varṣaṃ dine dātavyaṃ(vyam) ||

------------------------------------------------------

{1} From impressions.
{2} Denoted by a symbol. Of the figures that follow, those of thousand and hundred are damaged and the 
	first of these is engraved as joined to the point after saṃ. What appears between them is only a scratch 
	on the stone. 
{3} The daṇḍas are redundant. In his List referred to above, Kielhorn read the first two letters in this line 
	as given here and remarked that the original text has perhaps -kula. But the impression before me
	shows the traces of putra as taken here. 
{4} The bracketed letters are lost and have been restored from Jackson's reading. 
{5} The impressions show a curve above this letter, suggesting that it may have been tri, an abbreviation of 
	trivedin. 
{6} This is an abbreviation of dramma.
{7} This letter appears to be ch, and in view of the following letters we have to read it as cch(च्छ).
