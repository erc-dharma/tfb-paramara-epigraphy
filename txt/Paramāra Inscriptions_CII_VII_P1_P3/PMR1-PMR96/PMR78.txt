{1} From impressions and subsequent personal scrutiny.
{2} One expects here a symbol denoting auspeciousness, as also shown by the traces.
{3} The first two digits, which are obliterated and illegible in the impressions, are adopted here from 
	Bhandarkar's reading. The second akṣara of the name of the month is clearly g with a repha above,
	and hence I take the month as Mārga (śīrṣa) and not Māgha as read by him.
{4} The two akṣaras appear to be a struck off in the original and the whole corrected as prasādāvāpta. 
{5} The rest of the inscription is illegible.
