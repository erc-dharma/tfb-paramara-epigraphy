Text: Prose and Metrical in 8 verses
Metres: Verses 1-2 and 4-5 Anuṣṭubh; vv.3 and 7 Vasantatilakā; v.6 Indravajrā; v.8 Śālinī

Text{1}

1 oṃ{2} svasti | ja[yobhyudayaśca | jayati*]{3} vyomakeśosau [yaḥ sarggāya vi(bi)bhartti tām | ai*n]davīṃ si(śi)rasā lekhāṃ jaga-
2 [dvī(dbī)]jāṃkurākṛtim ||1|| tanvaṃtu{4} vaḥ smarārāteḥ kalyāṇamaniśaṃ jaṭāḥ | kalpānta samayoddāmataḍi-
3 dvalaya piṅgalāḥ ||2|| paramabhaṭṭāraka mahārājādhirāja parameśvara śrī vākpatirājadeva pā[dānu*]-
4 dhyāta paramabhaṭṭāraka mahārājādhirāja parameśvara śrī sindhurājadeva pā[dānudhyāta para*]-
5 ma bhaṭṭāraka mahārājādhirāja parameśvara śrī bhojadeva [pādānudhyāta para]{5}[mabhaṭṭāraka ma*]-
6 hārājādhirāja parameśvara śrī udayādityadeva[ḥ*]{6} kuśalī || caccuroṇī maṃḍa[lāntaḥpāti..]{7} -
7 radrahadvādaśake śrī kośavarddhanadurggīya śrī somanāthadeva [bhu]kteranu{8} ......
8 vilāpadraka grāme samupagatān samasta rājapuruṣān vrā(brā)hmaṇottarān prati[nivāsi paṭṭakila ja*]
9 napadādīṃś ca vo(bo)dhayatyastu vaḥ saṃviditaṃ ||{9} yathā karppāsikā grāmāvasthitair asmā[bhi------dhi*]{10}
10 kaśataikādaśa saṃvatsare caitra su(śu)di caturddaśyāṃ damanaka parvvaṇi snātvā carācara gu[ruṃ bhagavantaṃ bha*]-
11 vānīpatiṃ samabhyarccya saṃsārasyāsāratāṃ dṛṣṭvā | tathā hi | vātābhra vibhramam idaṃ vasudhādhipa[tyam āpāta*]-
12 mātra madhuro viṣayopabhogaḥ | prāṇās tṛṇāgra jala vi(bi)ndu samā narāṇāṃ dharmmaḥ sakhā parama[ho para*]-
13 loka yāne ||3|| bhramat saṃsāra cakrāgra dhārā dhārām imāṃ śriyaṃ(yam) | prāpya ye na dadusteṣāṃ paścāttāpaḥ paraṃ phalaṃ(lam ) [||4||*]
14 iti jagato vinaśvaraṃ svarūpamākalayyādṛṣṭa phalam aṃgīkṛtya candrārkkārṇṇava kṣiti sama kālaṃ yā-
15 vat parayā bhaktyā śrī kośa varddhana dugrggīya śrī somanāthadevāyaivoparilikhita grāmaḥ sa(sva) sīmā tṛṇa yū-
16 ti gocara paryantaḥ savṛkṣa mālā kulaḥ sahiraṇya bhāga bhogoparikara sarvvādāya sametaś ca mātṛ(tā)pitror ātma-
17 naś ca puṇya yaśobhivṛddhaye |{11} śāsanenodaka pūrvvakatayā pradatta iti | tanmatvā tannivāsi janapadair yathā [pra]-
18 dīyamāna bhāga bhogakara hiraṇyādikaṃ deva vrā(brā)hmaṇa bhukti varjjam ājñā śravaṇa vidheyair bhūtvā sarvvamamuṣmai
19 samupanetavyaṃ(vyam) | sāmānyaṃ caitat puṇya phalaṃ vu(bu)ddhvā asmad vaṃśajair aṇyai(nyai)r api bhāvi bhoktṛbhir asmat pradatta dharmmā-{12}
20 dāyoyam anumaṃtavyaḥ pālanīyaś ca || uktaṃ ca | va(ba)hubhir vvasudhā bhuktā rājabhiḥ sagarādibhiḥ | yasya ya-
21 sya yadā bhūmis tasya tasya tadā phalaṃ(lam) ||5|| yānīha dattāni purā narendrair ddānāni dharmmārtha yaśas karāṇi | ni-
22 rmmālya vānti pratimāni tāni ko nāma sādhuḥ punar ādadīta ||6|| asmat kula kramam udāramudāharadbhir aṇyai(nyai)-
23 ś ca dānamidam abhyanumodanīyaṃ(yam) | lakṣmyās taḍid valaya vudvu(budbu)da caṃcalāyā dānaṃ phalaṃ parayaśaḥ paripāla-
24 naṃ ca ||7|| sarvvān etān bhāvinaḥ pārthiveṃdrān bhūyo bhūyo yācate rāmabhadraḥ | sāmānyoyaṃ dharmma setur nṛ{13} --------

{1} From the impression.
{2} Expressed by a symbol which is partly visible.
{3} The portion in the brackets has totally peeled off and the reading is conjectural, following Dr.Alterkar, who
	observed that svasti is generally followed by jayati vyomakeśosau in the records of the Paramāras. The space
	can also accommodate these letters.
{4} The sign of anusvāra is partly visible above va, and the consonant of ta that follows is cut as bha.
{5} These seven akṣaras are now entirely lost and have been adopted here from Dr. Altekar's reading.
{6} Read śrī-Udayāditya-. We have some other instances of the type where sandhi, though necessary, has not been
	observed for the sake of clarity.
{7} The portion lost here contained six akṣaras, four of which are as supplied here and the last two may have been
	kaka, to make the complete name Kakaradraha, for the identification of which see above.
{8} The rest of the line is lost.
{9} The daṇḍas are redundant.
{10} Here, four letters are lost, obviously showing the unit and the decimal figures, in words.
{11} The daṇḍas in the line are superfluous.
{12} This expression appears also in some other grants and is current throughout Mālwā even to this day. It may
	 be interpreted as dharmasya ādāyaḥ ādānam, grahaṇam.
{13} The rest of the inscription is lost, as already stated above.
