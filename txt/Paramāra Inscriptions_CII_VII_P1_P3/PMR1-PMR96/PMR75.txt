{1} From an impression.
{2} Read parvvaṇi. From māgha up to this word, the construction is as in the Anuṣṭubh metre.
{3} Read yaśodhavaladeva--.
{4} The daṇḍa is redundant.
{5} This is a suffix meaning 'of'; or it may be the initial letter of rāṇā.
{6} The Sanskrit equivalent of this name is Lakṣmaṇasiṃha. The expression that follows is unintelligible
	to me.
{7} All the letters in this line are visible only in their lower parts and hence they cannot be made out.
{8} The first akṣara has the mātrā of ō above, and hence its reading as hā and taking the whole word as
	mahārātrē to denote śivarātri, as taken by Halder, is not acceptable. There is also a redundant repha
	on tre.
{9} The repha appears to have been omitted. Or, it is lost in the original, as shown by a trace above.
{10} Read kṣaye kṣīṇe.
{11} What appears to be intended is pādalagnosmi.
{12} Fill up the gap by reading yet, which has left traces. Perhaps dattiṃ (a gift) is intended here.
{13} Read mahī--.
