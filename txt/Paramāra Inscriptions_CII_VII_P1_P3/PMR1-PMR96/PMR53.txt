{1} From the inked impression.
{2} Expressed by symbol.
{3} After this numeral there are some faint traces leading us to doubt whether the tithi was intended to be 30,
	but on that tithi there was a Tuesday and not Thursday, as mentioned in the inscription.
{4} The impression shows this akṣara preceded by another which appears to belong to another inscription,
	incised on its left. In the next line also we have the same case.
{5} The last akṣara in this line is indistinct and so are the last two akṣaras of the next line, which are all
	represented here by equal number of dots.
{6} The letter in the brackets looks like pa, but I take it as ma as shown by the name of the village. The
	vowel u is followed by a horizontal stroke as also the same akṣara in 1.3, above. Read-taḥ.
{7} It is not known if the mātrā of this akṣara is scored off in the original.
{8} Part of this akṣara along with the sign of visarga, if cut all, has disappeared. From traces left, the
	lost letters in this line may have been mahādvādaśakamaṃḍale. Also seee Ind. Ant., Vol.XVIII,p.344.
{9} In my examination of the original, I read the first letter of the name as ṣa for kha, and thus the whole
	name is kharehī, as also in the preceding inscription. The reading madhye is also uncertain, and more likely
	pathake appears to have been engraved here.
{10} Possibly what is intended is devadharmapuryāṃ. The third akṣara of this name is distorted.
{11} The backeted akṣara has an unnecessary vertical stroke on either side.
{12} This and the following line, which are the last two lines of the record, are indistinct.
