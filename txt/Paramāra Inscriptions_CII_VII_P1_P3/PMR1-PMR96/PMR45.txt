{1} As in Journ. of Asiatic Society of Bengal, Vol.VII, pp.736-39. The figures numbering the lines are
	given by me. The transcript also contains some redundant punctuation marks.
{2} As in many Paramāra grants, there appears to have been an auspicious symbol at the beginning, which has
	been omitted in the transcript. Read tāṃ and kṛtiṃ respectively at the end of each of the hemistiches.
	It is possible that in both the words in this verse and in similar cases below, the consonants v and b may
	have been engraved. But we cannot be definite about it.
{3} The rule of sandhi is not observed here.
{4} This expression is apparently a mistake for pratigrāmanivāsinaśca. Also read amarāpadra in the preceding line,
	and delete the mātrā of ke in viṣayike in the next line.
{5} Read jātavedaderdhyaṃ.
{6} For these great gifts, see Hemādri, Chaturvargachintāmaṇi, Dānakhaṇḍa (Ban. edn.) Vol. I, pp.256 ff.
{7} Delete one of the daṇḍas.
{8} In the transcript, this verse is numbered one, and the initial two verses are not numbered. I have my own 
	arrangement of numbering the verses, as necessary.
{9} The punctuation marks are redundant.
{10} This is evidently a printing mistake for suta, though grammatically correct.
{11} The name of the village is given as palasavāḍā in 1.10, above as savāḍā in 1.30, below.
{12} Read ṣaṭtriṃśadadhika-.
{13} Read śā for sa and separate this word from the preceding one.
{14} Read -vyam. Below, as here, there are instances with the anusvāra for ṃ at the end of a sentence or a
	 hemistich; these are not pointed out separately.
{15} This expression generally appears as dharmamahaṃ in the inscriptions.
{16} THis line which is not a part of the original inscription, was evidently written in a later age by somebody.
