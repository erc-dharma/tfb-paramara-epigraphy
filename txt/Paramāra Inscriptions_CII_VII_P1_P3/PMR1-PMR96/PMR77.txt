PennGlobal: PMR77
Period: VS 1277 māgha śudi 9
Location: Dhāntā
Dynasty: Paramāra
King: Somasiṃha
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose
Gist: Records the installation of an image, 
	  on the pedestal of which it is engraved during the reign of Somasiṃha
Published reference: Trivedi (1978), no. 77.


Text{1}

1. siddham{2} | (saṃ)vatu(t) 1277 va[rṣe] māha{3} su(śu)di 9 so[me] śrīcaṃdrāvatyāṃ ||{4}
2. śrīsā[e] masi(siṃ)havijayarājye dhā[ṇa]tāgrāme śrīsīgāradevi-
3. ..{5}kāle pravarta[māne] de[vyāḥ mūrttiḥ]{6} kārāpitā{7} pra..{8}
4. .......................... ||

----------------------------------------------

{1} From the facsimile facing page 211 in Ep. Ind., Vol. XXXVII.
{2} Expressed by a symbol which is partly visible.
{3} Read māgha.
{4} The daṇḍas are superflous.
{5} These two akṣaras cannot be made out. The first of them appears to be bh (?). THe first letter of
	the name is perhaps sīṃ on the original.
{6} The reading of the bracketed letters is conjectural, from the traces left.
{7} Incorrect for kāritā, as we find also in many other inscriptions of the time. 
{8} The intended reading is perhaps pratiṣṭhā for pratiṣṭhāpitā. All the letters in the line that follows are
	partially visible.
