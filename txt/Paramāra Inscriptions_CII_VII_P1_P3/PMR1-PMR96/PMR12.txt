{1} From photolithographs facing pp. 52-54 in Ind. Ant., Vol.VI.
{2} Denoted by a symbol.
{3} This akṣara appears as na(न).
{4} By scribal error, this akṣara appears as da(द).
{5} The daṇḍas are superfluous.
{6} The curve of the ī-mātrā is not carved and sandhi is not made with the preceding ā(आ).
{7} This and some other daṇḍas below are redundant, which need not be mentioned separately.
{8} What is intetnded here by the word kalpita(कल्पित) is not definitely known. This term occurs also in the Kadamba-
	padraka grant of Naravarman of V.S. 1110 and in his son Yaśovarman's grant of V.S. 11192; and while 
	has to be taken in the sense of 'settled or fixed'. And the whole expression may perhaps here refer to the 
	donated village, the halas of which were fixed and also entered in lesya(लेस्य) i.e., the government documents.
	I am, however, unable to explain, in this case, the use of the seventh case in the word lekhya(लेख्य).
{9} The sign manual, which is engraved on the right corner of the first plate, should not be taken to break the con-
	 tinuity of the writing.
{10} The meaning of this word I cannot make out. Kiratanē translates it as 'inhabitant of sādhasurasaṃga(साधसुरसंग)-'but to me
	 it appears to be a mistake for dharāsura(धरासुर), a Brāhmaṇa, or, as Hultzch has suggested, in Ep. Ind., Vol. XI,
	 p.184, n.4, we may have to read here vinirggata(||) bhūsurasaṃga (विनिर्ग्गत(||)भूसुरसंग). For the double daṇḍas which are as carved
	 here, cf. above, 1.2, after jaṭāḥ(जटाः).
{11} This akṣara is followed by a small hollow circle.
