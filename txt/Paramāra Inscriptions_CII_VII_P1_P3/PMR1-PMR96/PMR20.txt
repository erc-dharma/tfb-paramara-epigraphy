Text{1}

1. saṃ{2} 1138 | [ja]saharaḥ{3} [a]{4}gnichi-
2. tā{5} prana(ṇa)[ma]tiḥ{6} lāravarg{7} |

--------------------------------------------
{1} From facsimile accompanying Lele's article and from personal examination.
{2} The tail of the fore-part of this akṣara is missing. Lele read the akṣara without the anusvāra which is clear
	above.
{3} Lele suggested the bracketed letter to be read as da, and took the whole word as dasahara, i.e., dasaharā, giving
	the day. But agreeing with Garde's suggestion, I take it as given here and thus showing the name
	of the consecrator, which is otherwise missing.
{4} This akṣara was taken as bha by Lele who corrected it to a. But to me it appears to be a misformed a.
{5} Read agnisthitāṃ, or rather agnimadhyasthitāṃ, which is the description of Pārvati performing penance for obtaining
	Śiva as her husband. cf. Kumāra-Saṃbhava, Canto V.
{6} Drop the sign of visarga.
{7} Read -varggaḥ. The va is misformed and the repha is shown by a vertical small stroke above.
