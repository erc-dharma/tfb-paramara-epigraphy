PennGlobal: PMR71
Period: VS 1245 bhādrapada śudi 1
Location: Muṅgthalā
Dynasty: Paramāra
King: Dhārāvarṣa
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose
Gist: Inscription records an order of Dhārāvarshadēva 
	  to grow an orchard in the village of Phīlini in the temple.
Published reference: Trivedi (1978), no. 71.


Text{1}

1. Siddham{2} || saṃ[va]t 1245 bhādrapada sū(śu)di 1 vu(bu)dhe || adyeha caṃdrā-
2. vatyāṃ māṃ[ḍa]la[i]kasuraśaṃbhuḥ{3} śrīdhārāvarṣadevaḥ [kṣetrapa]kṛ{4}-
3. tyamakṣarāṇi{5} likhāpayati yathā | śrīvasiṣṭhāśrame phīli-
4. ṇigrāme{6} maṭhe ca yaḥ kopi bhaṭṭha(ṭṭa)putro bhavati tena śrīvaśi- ||{7}
5. ṣṭā(ṣṭhā)śrame phīliṇigrāmavāṭikāyāṃ vasaṃti[ṣu] mātarāya |{8}
6. caṃpaka | phaṇasa | cārākara[mva]rā{9} pradatā(ttā) gāṃ(gām) aśoka | va(ba)kula |
7. nāraṃgī | dhāṃdhala [|] priyā-śatapatrikā prabhṛti dṛkvā(vṛkṣā)ṇāṃ{10} vī(bī)jā- ||
8. ni kṛṣṇagaṃḍukāni vṛkṣāṇāṃ jāta(ru ?)dhiñca{11}  prativa[rṣaṃ] vāvāpa- |
9. nīyā(ni) varddha(ropī)yāni{12} ca | īdra(dṛ)śaṃ na ku[rvvaṃ]ti{13} tadā bhadva(ṭṭa ?){14} |
10. putrāḥ [syā(śvā)]naṃ{15}(na)garddabhacāṃḍa[ā*]lavat rāṇakaiḥ nigrahaṇīyā[ḥ*] ||
11. daṃḍanīyāśca || tasya mātā garddabhoyaṃ draṣṭavyaḥ ||{16}

----------------------------------------------------

{1} From impressions.
{2} Expressed by a symbol.
{3} To be restored to māṇḍalikeśvaraśambhuḥ.
{4} The reading of the bracketed letters is doubtful. The first of these looks more like si and the third
	like ya.
{5} Probably -tye akṣarāṇi. The second of these letters is damaged. The sign of the mātrā of khā that follows
	seems to have been engraved subsequently.
{6} The mātrā of the first letter of the name is partly visible.
{7} The punctuation marks are redundant.
{8} The punctuation mark, here and in some lines below, is redundant, and the language used here is corrupt.
	The akṣara in the brackets also appears as ṣva; it is damaged.
{9} The reading of the first two akṣaras here is uncertain and they also appear as cāyaṃ; and that of the 
	letter in the brackets is also equally uncertain. The punctuation marks in this and the following lines
	too are superfluous. Phaṇasa, wrongly written for panasa, is a bread fruit tree or jack tree, for which
	see I. E. G., p.402.
{10} Of the trees mentioned here, the first is local, thriving in Rajasthan, the second is a kind of jasmine
	 and the third is probably a lotus.
{11} Probably to be restored to -gaṃḍa(dha?)kādi-. The meaning of these four akṣaras is not known to me and they
	 seem to be restored to jātiṃ vṛddhiṃ ca. The bracketed letter that follows is damaged and may also be
	 read as rṣe.
{12} Corrupt. The preceding ni was at first engraved as mi and corrected subsequently.
{13} The sign of repha is faintly visible on the stone.
{14} I am not certain about the reading of the conjunct consonant of this akṣara which appears to have been
	 subsequently corrected and has also the sign of the secondary ā attatched to it, probably intended for a 
	 daṇḍa. Bhaḷḷa means a master, a Brāhmaṇa or a bard. In the Tilakamañjarī this word is used to 
	 denote a messenger (p.112), i.e., a general type of servant, and following this, we may take the word to 
	 denote a gardener. This word is mentioned in the Lekhapaddhati, (15, 20) apparently in the sense of a 
	 soldier, and also in I. N. I., No. 361, to denote a royal officer (rāja-puruṣa).
{15} The sign of anusvāra is lightly rubbed off on the stone so as to leave its mark. Also of gardabha-
	 chaṇḍālanyāyena in the Lekhapaddhati, G. O. S., No. XXI, Glossary.
{16} Below the inscription is the ass-motif.
