PennGlobal: PMR58
Period: VS 1320 vaiśākha śudi 3
Location: Vidiśā
Dynasty: Paramāra
King: Jayasiṃha
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose
Gist: Gift of a woman for the spiritual merit of her husband, 
	  refers to the Bhāilasvāmideva temple  
Published reference: Trivedi (1978), no. 58.



Text{1}

1. siddham{2} || saṃvat 1320 varṣe vaiśāṣa(kha)su(śu)di [3]{3} gurau adye[ha]
2. [śrībhāi]{4}lsvāmidevapure śrījayasiṃhade[*va]rājye pubhā{5}-
3. [ni]nnā(nā)yaka paṃ ṭha{6} madanasī(siṃ)-
4. ha[de*]va śreya(yo)nimitta(ttaṃ) ku-
5. ptakā[stha]devī sānu-
6. matī{7} dvormelaṃ pra-
7. dattaṃ(ttam |) yo na da{8}-
8. dāti tasya mā-
9. tā{9} gardabho ja-
10. bhāti{10} ||

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

{1} From facsimile facing p.187 in Ep.Ind., Vol.XXXV.
{2} Expressed by symbol, the formation of which is rather peculiar and not generally found in inscriptions.
{3} Sircar remarked that the reading of this numeral may be 2 also, but then it would offend against the 
	week-day (Thursday) mentioned just after it and which, according to him, is regular.
{4} At the beginning of this line there is a trace of one akṣara which I am unable to make out. It appears
	as su (?)
{5} This akṣara appears also as ptā, and thus the reading is uncertain. Compare with it the formation of 
	the first akṣara in 1.5. However, I follow Sircar in the reading of this name as also of two others
	which occur in II.4-6.
{6} That is, Paṇḍita-Ṭhakkura.
{7} As Sircar has already suggested, read devyā sānumatyā. The reading of the first akṣara of the name, however,
	appears to me to be doubtful, as it may also have been bhā.
{8} What appears after this akṣara may not have been a redundant daṇḍa, as taken by Sircar, but a kāka-
	pada sign, which is partly preserved.
{9} Read mātaraṃ.
{10} Read yabhati. The double daṇḍa that follows is joined at the top by a horizontal stroke.
