{1} From photographs and impressions. The plates are numbered here as they were found.
{2} It is an epithet of Yama, and here it means inflicting torture on his enemies.
{3} Read vakṣāṃsi, but it would not suit the metre. The first word of this quarter may better be restored to dagdhvā.
{4} Kudalkar: surāditya; but the medial vowel attached to the first akṣara of this name is long, as to be used
	in some other instances in this inscription, e.g. in 1.6, below.
{5} This vowel ought to have been dropped, as required by the rules of sandhi, but it would not suit the metre.
{6} What is probably intended is kṣīṇaśūrāṇāṃ, in the sense of 'one who destroyed his enemies'; and accordingly,
	the name of the hero appears to be śūrāditya, i.e., shining like the Sun amongst the brave.
{7} This pāsa offends against the metre.
{8} Probably amātyaṃ or amātyān and (svīyān) putrān is intended and not 'sons of (his) ministers', as Kudalkar
	translates on p.324. ca is misplaced.
{9} Read vidito vaḥ prākramaḥ.
{10} This letter, if amended to vai, would remove the difficulty of the wrong use of the plural number here.
{11} Restore to gurubhiranujñātaḥ, but this would offend against the metre.
{12} Read-grāhakastatra.
{13} Read dharmmaseturnṛpāṇāṃ.
{14} Thus punctuation mark is redundant.
{15} Read rājabhiḥ
{16} Read ṣaṣṭirvarṣa or ṣaṣṭiṃ varṣa or ṣaṣṭivarṣa
{17} Read kṛṣṇasarpā abhijāyante. Sandhi is wrongly observed here for metrical exigencies. Also read bhūmiharttāraḥ in
	the last pāda.
{18} This letter should be long. The word vālabhya appears to be connected with valabhī. It may also be 
	interesting to note that the sign for bh used here varies from that of the same letter in the name of the composer
	in the following line. For the two forms of this letter, see my remarks above in the palaeographical section of
	this article.
{19} To be restored to śobhik(?)
{20} The last quarter of this verse is merely pāda-pūrti. The construction too is wrong.
