1. sāmānyaṃ caitat puṇya phalaṃ vu(bu)ddhvāsmad vaṃśajair anyair api bhāvi bhoktṛbhir asmat pradatta dharmma dāyoya-
2. ma[nu]ga(ma)ntavyappā(vyaḥ pā)lamī(nī)yaś a(ś ca) [|*] uktaṃ ca bhaga[va*]tā vyāsena [|*] va(ba)hubhir vvasudhā bhuktā rājabhiḥ
  sagarādibhiḥ | yasya yasya
3. yadā bhūmi[s t]sya tasya tadā phalaṃ(lam) |[|1||*] yānīha dattāni purā nare[n*]ddrair ddanāni dharmmārtha yaśaṣ ka(s ka)rāṇi [|*] nimmā(rmma ) -
4. lyavā[ntaḥ]{2}pratimāni tāni ko nāma sādhuṣpu(dhuḥ pu)nar ādadīta ||[2||*] asmat kulakramam udāram udāharadbhir anyai-
5. śca dāma(na)m idam abhyanumodanīyaṃ(yam) | lakṣmyāst{3}aḍit salila vudvu(budbu)da ca(caṃ)calāyāḥ{4} dāna(naṃ) phalaṃ parayaśa-
6. ṣpa(ḥ pa)ripālanaṃ ca ||[3||*] sarvvān etān bhāvinaṣ pā(ḥ pā)rthi[ve]ndrādbhū(n bhū)yo bhūyo yāce(ca)te rāma bhadraḥ | sāmā[nyo]{5}-
7. ya(yaṃ) dharmma{6} setur nṛpāṇāṃ |{7} kāle kāle pālanīyo bhavadbhiḥ ||[4||*] iti kamala dalāmvu(mbu){8} uvi(bi)ndu lolāṃ [śri]-
8. yam anucintya manuṣya jīvita(taṃ) ca |{9} sakalamidam udāhṛta(taṃ) ca vu(bu)ddhvā na hi puruṣai{10}ṣpa(ṣaiḥ pa)ra kīrtta-
9. yo vilopyāḥ |[|5||*] iti | saṃ 1026 āśvina vadi 15 [|*] svayam ājñā | dāpakaś cātra kanhapaikaḥ |
10. śrīsīyakasya sva hastoyaṃ(yam) |
