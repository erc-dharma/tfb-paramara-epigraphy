{1} From Kielhorn's transcript in Ind. Ant., Vol. XIV, pp.477 f.
{2} Keilhorn: मधुरश्वासैः (madhuraśvāsaiḥ). Both the corrections as given by him and by me are equally good.
{3} Read either पौर्ण्णमास्यां(paurṇṇamāsyāṃ) or पूर्णिमायां (pūrṇimāyāṃ).
{4} Sandhi is optional here.
{5} This expression has been variously interpreted. Colebrooke rendered it by "within the proper bounds,
    extending to the grass and pasture" (Misc. Essays, Vol. II, p.312); and Kirtane translated it by "within all its
    proper boundaries, which extend as far as the ground for the tending of the cattle and for cutting grass"
    (Ind. Ant., Vol. VI, p.53). Referring to both these renderings, Kielhorn translated the expression as "up
    to its proper boundaries, the grass and pasture land" (op. cit., p.161). The purport of all these renderings
    is the same, but all these scholars have failed to catch the meaning of the word yuti, which means 'mixing' or
    'junction'. And thus I would render the expression as 'extending up to the grass within its bounds and the 
    pasture land connected with it.'
{6} This word means 'show' or 'spectacle'. Possibly pro(प्रो) may have been intended.
{7} Kielhorn: "putting in order of the temple buildings, when damaged or out of repairs." I would, however,
    take it as khaṇḍaśaḥ sphuṭita (खण्डशः स्फुटित), partially broken, and also for the construction of jagatī(जगती), foundation, or for repairing 
    the jagatī(जगती), which was partially broken.
{8} This word appears to be restored to asyai(अस्यै).
{9} Read sagarādibhiḥ | yasya -.(सगरादिभिः | यस्य -.)
{10} Read vilopyāḥ ||7|| iti (विलोप्याः ||७|| इति).
{11} Read svayamājñā | dāpakaścātra. For translation, see n. on the same in the preceding inscription.
