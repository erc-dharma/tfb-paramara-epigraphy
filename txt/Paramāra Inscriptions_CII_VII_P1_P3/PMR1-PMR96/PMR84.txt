Gist: Records the line of kings belonging to the subordinate Paramāra house ruling over the province of Sthāli or Vāgaḍa. 
	  Records the foundation of a temple, Maṇḍalēśa. Also discusses the maintenance of the temple, including imports and exports; 
	  What is sold at the bazaar, etc.
Published reference: Trivedi (1978), no. 84.
