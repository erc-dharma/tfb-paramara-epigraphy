{1} From the original plates
{2} Expressed by a symbol
{3} Ojha read this letter as ṇe(णे) and corrected it to ṇo(णो); but the sign of medial ā is distinct on the plate.
{4} These strokes are redundant.
{5} It may also be read as gavi(गवि).
{6} Read saṃvṛttaṃ(संवृत्तं)I am not definite but the whole phrase possibly means 'from the ordinary (level) land of
	the village, a prastha (a kind of measure) consisting of thirty-four shares and worthy to be tilled by
	four ploughs'. The use of saṃvṛttaṃ(संवृत्तं) is not quite clear. Should we take the word derived from vṛt, to
	turn (to plough?).
{7} This and some of the following daṇḍas are redundant.
{8} Following bho(भो), a letter appears to have been originally formed but left uncarved. The sign manual need
	not break the continuity of the writing.
{9} The visarga is redundant.
{10} Ojha:cadrā(caṃdrā) (चद्रा(चंद्रा)); but the sign for n(न्)is faintly visible.
{11} Originally ci(चि) was engraved and subsequently the ūrdhva-mātrā was also carved without scoring off the
	 unnecessary parts.
{12} Originally pa(प), latter on corrected to pha(फ).
{13} The visarga is not dropped here.
