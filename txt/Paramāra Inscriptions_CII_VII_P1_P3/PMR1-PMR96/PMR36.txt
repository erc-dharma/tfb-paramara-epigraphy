{1} From impressions.
{2} Expressed by a symbol.
{3} The second letter of the name is mutilated and I am not certain whether it is superscribed by ra,
	whereas in 1.14 below, the reading is clearly carccikā.
{4} The fourth letter in this line is totally lost, leaving only faint traces thereof and the third and the fifth
	syllables are mutilated. The consonant of the third letter is definitly y(य्). Thus it is difficult to be 
	certain about the reading of these three letters.
{5} The word labdhi, acquisition, needs the construction of the sentence in the passive voice.
{6} By mistake, two horizontal strokes instead of one are attached to the vertical of this letter.
{7} This akṣara, which was omitted while engraving, is later on written above, at the close of the preceding
	line, with an arrow-mark to draw attention to it.
{8} Cf. parakulāṃganāputra used for the Rāṣṭrakūṭa King Kṛṣṇa III in Ep. Ind., Vol. XIX, pp.289-90.
{9} The punctuation mark is so close to the preceding letter as to appear as a mātrā attached to it. It is also
	redundant, as some others in the lines that follow.
