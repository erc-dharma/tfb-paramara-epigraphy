Text{1}

1. svasti || eka cchattrāṃ karotu kṣmām udayāditya bhūpatiḥ |
2. ityādyaṃ siddhidaṃ vedaṃ śaṃsāmaḥ sarvvato nṛpa{2} ||1
3. kṣmā si(śi)rasi bhūtyā sa kṣmā bhūd yaśastu{3} | || ravi saṃkrāti-
4. karaṇaṃ(ṇam) || [śloko]yaṃ paṃḍita śrī śṛṅgavāsa sūnoḥ |{4}
5. paṃḍita śrī mahīpālasya || saṃva[t] 1137 vaisā(śā)kha sudi 7{5}
6. śrī madudayesva(śva)radevasya dhvajārohaḥ saṃpūrṇaḥ | ma[ṃ]galaṃ mahāśrīḥ ||

----------------------------------------------------
{1} From the original and the impression.
{2} The use of this word appears to be in imitation of the writings of old in which some king is addressed.
{3} The meaning of this expression is not clear.
{4} The daṇḍa is redundant.
{5} This is a pious day when the Ganges is known to have descended in this world.
