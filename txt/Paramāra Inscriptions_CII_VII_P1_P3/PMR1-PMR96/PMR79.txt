{1} From an impression.
{2} The number of akṣaras lost after this akṣara cannot be made out.
{3} That is somasiṃha-. The sign of anusvāra is faint even on the stone.
{4} This akṣara is probably ya, which was obviously followed by rājye, which too appears to be lost. 
{5} The rest of the record is all in the local dialect and I am unable to make out the sense.
