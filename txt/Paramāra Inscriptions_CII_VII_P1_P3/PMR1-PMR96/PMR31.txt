PennGlobal: PMR31
Period: VS 1152 bhādrapada śudi 11
Location: Dēwās
Dynasty: Paramāra
King: Naravarman
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose and Metrical in 4 verses
Metres: Verse 1-2 and 4 Anuṣṭubh; v.3 Vasantatilakā
Gist: Land donation to brāhmaṇas by the king Naravarman
Published reference: Trivedi (1978), no. 31.


Text{1}

1 om{2} svasti | jayobhyudayaśca || jayati vyomakeśo{3}sau yaḥ sarggāya vi(bi)bhārtti tāṃ(tām) | aiṃdavīṃ śirasā lekhāṃ jaga-
2 d vī(d bī)jāṃkurākṛtim ||1|| tanvantu vaḥ smarārāteḥ kalyāṇamaniśaṃ jaṭāḥ | kalpānta samayoddāma taḍid valaya piṅga-
3 lāḥ ||[2*] parama bhaṭṭāraka mahārājādhirāja parameśvara śrī siṃdhurāja deva pādānudhyāta parama bhaṭṭāraka ma-
4 hārājādhirāja parameśvara śrī bhojadeva pādānudhyāta parama bhaṭṭāraka mahārājādhirāja parameśvara-
5 śrī{4}udayādityadeva pādānudhyāta parama bhaṭṭāraka mahārājādhirāja parameśvara śrīnaravarmmadevaḥ ku-
6 śalī || iṅgaṇīpadra sārddhasaptaśata bhoge bhagava{5}tpura prati jāgaraṇake śrī dharmmādhikaraṇe(ṇa) paṃḍita prabhāka—
7 ra bhujyamāna bhū hala paṃcaka varjjaṃ mālāpurake grāme samupagatān samasta rājapu-
8 ruṣān vrā(n brā)hmaṇottarān pratinivāsi paṭṭakila janapadādīṃś ca vo(bo)dhayatyastu vaḥ saṃviditaṃ(tam) || yathā cāmaṭikā{6} -
9 grāmāvasthitair asmābhiḥ dvipaṃcāśadadhikaśataikādaśa saṃvatsare bhādrapaṃ(pa)da su(śu)di ekādasyāṃ(śyāṃ) mahārāja-
10 śrī udayādityadeva sāmva(sāṃva)tsarike revānadī kuvilārā nadyoḥ{7} saṃgame snātvā carācaraguruṃ bhagava[ṃ]taṃ
11 bhavānīpatiṃ śrīnīlakaṃṭhadevaṃ samabhyarccya saṃsārasyāsāratāṃ dṛṣṭvā tathā hi {8}vātābhra vibhramamidaṃ vasu-
12 dhādhipatyam āpātamātra madhuro viṣayopabhogaḥ |{9} prāṇās tṛṇāgra jala viṃdu(bindu) samā narāṇāṃ dharmmaḥ sakhā
13 paramaho paraloka yāne ||[3*] bhramat saṃsāra cakrāgra dhārā dhārāmimāṃ śriyaṃ(yam) [|*] prāpya ye na dadusteṣāṃ paścāttāpaḥ pa-
14 raṃ phalam || [4* ] iti jagato vinasca(śva)raṃ {10}sca(sva)rūpamākalayyādṛ[ṣṭa]{11} phalamaṅgīkṛtya caṃdrārkkārṇṇava kṣiti sama kālaṃ yā-
15 vat parayā bhaktyā dakṣiṇā pathāntaḥ pāti adriyalavidāsthāna vinirggata bhāradvāja [go]tra bhāradvāja āṃgirasa-
16 vā(bā)rha[spatye]ti tri pravara āśvalāyana śākhādhyāyi vrā(brā)hmaṇa dhanapāla pautra {12}ma[hi]rasvāmi suta viśvarūpāya
17 upari likhita mālāpuraka grāmāt ṣaṇṇavati parvva daṇḍa prāmāṇyenobhaya dvicatvāriṃśan māpyakena
18 [bhū]nivarttana viṃśati prathā māpita bhū hala dvayan tathā asminneva samva(saṃva)tsare nityakalpita lekhye ......................

{1} From the original plate
{2} Expressed by a symbol
{3} The palatal sibilant, wherever it occurs in this inscription, has its loop so curved as to appear as the mid-
	stroke of the dental, and the disstinction between the two letters can be noted only in a close study.
{4} Sandhi has not been performed here and also in 1.10 below, where the expression again occurs.
{5} Here and in some other cases below, which have not been pointed out separately, the letter v(व्) is engraved 
	as c(च्).
{6} The first letter of this name is somewhat like v(व्) or bh(भ्) or t(त्); but I take it as ca(च). The reading is
	doubtful. Near the confluence of the two rivers there was a village of the name of Camaradaha, sug-
	gesting its identification with Cāmaṭikā.
{7} Better read revākuvilārānadyoḥ.
{8} This verse and the following one are put by way of a paranthesis and the sentence is continued with 
	iti jagataḥ in 1.14.
{9} As the vacant space shows, this daṇḍa was originally engraved but has disappeared in the process of
	cutting the plate. The tra in mātra was originally engraved with three horizontal strokes and later on
	one of them was rubbed off.
{10} As stated above in n.6 this and the preceding v(व्) look like c(च्).
{11} The slanting bar of the member of the conjunct consonant is not engraved and thus it looks like p(प्).
{12} Sandhi is not performed in all the instances in this and the preceding lines, though compulsory; and the
	 top stroke showing the mātrā of pau is so engraved as to appear as the sign of the long medial ī.
