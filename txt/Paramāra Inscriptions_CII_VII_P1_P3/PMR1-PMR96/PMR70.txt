Location: Ajhārī
Dynasty: Paramāra
King: Dhārāvarṣa
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose
Gist: Refers to the victorious reigns of Dhārāvarṣa at Chandrāvatī. 
	  During his reign, a arahaṭṭa ( machine well) was donated by the prince Pālhaṇadeva, 
	  the younger brother of Dhārāvarṣa  and the crowned queen Sīgāradevi, 
	  (who was), The daughter of the Chāamāna donated a lamp.
Published reference: Trivedi (1978), no. 70.
