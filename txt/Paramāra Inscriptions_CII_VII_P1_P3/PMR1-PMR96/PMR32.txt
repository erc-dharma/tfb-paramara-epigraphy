Period: VS 1157 
Location: Bhojpūr
Dynasty: Paramāra
King: Naravarman
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose and Verse
Gist: The installation of two images Tīrthaṅkaras
Published reference: Trivedi (1978), no. 32.


Text{1}


1 sama(va)t{2} 1157 |{3} [śr]īnaravarmma svā(sā)mrājye vema{4} -
2 kānvaya{5}nemicaṃ[dra ?] sa(su)taḥ sre(śre)ṣṭhī rāmā[khyo] nū-
3 ṇi sutiyaḥ{6} [|*] tat putra cillaṇā[khye]na{7} jiva(na)-
4 yugmaṃ{8} pratiṣṭhitaṃ(tam) ||

---------------------------------------------------------------------
{1} From the impression supplied by the Chief Epigraphist.
{2} The horizontal bar of ma has a redundant chisel stroke, making the letter appear to be probably mva.
	Sircar read it as saṁ[va]t.
{3} This mark of punctuation is indicated by a visarga-like sign.--D.C.S.
{4} The reading of this and the preceding letter is doubtful, as of some others too.
{5} Sircar read: k-ānvaya(ye), followed by a daṇḍa; but it is not necessary if we take the daṇḍa to be super
	fluous and read it as a compound word.
{6} The intended reading may be munisupriyaḥ (D.C.S.)
{7} A wrong chisel stroke has made this letter look like ta.
{8} A faint trace below ya shows that the original has an u-mātrā, which has not come out in the impression.
