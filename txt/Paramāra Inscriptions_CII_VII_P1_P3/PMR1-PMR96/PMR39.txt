PennGlobal: PMR39
Period: VS c.1197 -1201
Location: Ujjain
Dynasty: Paramāra
King: Jayavarman
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose and Metrical in 4 verses
Metres: Vv. 1-2 and 4 Anuṣṭubh; v.3 Vasantatilakā.
Gist: Land donation announcement by Jayavarman.
Published reference: Trivedi (1978), no. 39.

Text{1}

1. om{2} svasti | śrīrjjayo'bhyudayaśca || jayati vyo{3}makeśo'sau yaḥ sarggāya{4} vi(bi)bhartti tā 
2. m | aiṃdavīṃ śirasā lekhāṃ jagadvī(dbī)jāṃkurākṛtim{5} ||1|| tanvaṃtu vaḥ smarārāteḥ kalyā-
3. ṇamaniśaṃ jaṭāḥ | kalpāntasamayoddāmataḍidvalayapiṃgalāḥ ||2|| śrīvarddhamāna-
4. purasamāvāsātpa{6}ramabhaṭṭārakamahārājādhirājaparameśvaraśrī{7}udayādityade-
5. vapādānudhyātaparamabhaṭṭārakamahārājādhirājaparameśvaraśrīnaravarmmadevapādānu-
6. dhyātaparamabhaṭṭārakamahārājādhirājaparameśvaraśrīyaśovarmmadevapādānudhyātapara-
7. mabhaṭṭārakamahārājādhirājaparameśvaraśrījayavarmmadevo vijayodayī{8} ||
8. vaṭakheṭakaṭtruṃ(triṃ)śatsaṃva(ba)ddhamāyamoḍakagrāme samastarājapuruṣānvrā(brā)hmaṇottarānpra-
9. tinivāsipaṭṭakilajanapadādīṃśca vo(bo)dhayatyastu vaḥ saṃviditaṃ yathā |{9} caṃdrapurīsamāvā -
10. sitairasmābhiḥ snātvā carācaraguruṃ bhagavaṃtaṃ bhavānīpatiṃ samabhyarccya saṃ{10}sārasyāsāratāṃ
11. dṛṣṭvā{11}| tathā hi | vātābhravibhramamidaṃ vasudhādhipatyamāpātamātramadhuro viṣayopabhoga-
12. ḥ | prāṇā[stṛ]ṇāgrajalavi(biṃ)dusamā narāṇāṃ dharmmaḥ sakhā paramaho paralokayāne ||3||
13. bhramatsaṃsāracakrāgradhārādhārāmimāṃ śriyaṃ(yam) | prāpya ye na dadusteṣāṃ paścāttāpaḥ paraṃ pha-
14. lam ||4|| iti jagato vinasva(śva)raṃ svarūpamākalayyādṛṣṭaphalamaṃgīkṛ[tyā]caṃdrā-
15. rkkā'rṇavakṣitisamakālaṃ yāvatparayā bhaktyā rājavra(bra)hmapuryā dakṣiṇadeśā-
16. ntaḥpāti{12} -adri[yala]viddhā{13}varīsthānavinirggatāya bhāradvāja ||{14} ......

{6} This akṣara appears as tra or tu. The word samāvāsāta is to be connected with vo(bo)dhayati in 1.9.

{12} As in n.11 on the preceding page.
{13} THis akṣara may also be read as dā, as Kielhorn takes it.
