PennGlobal: PMR28
Period: Śaka 1034 vaisākha śudi 10
Location: Ḍōṅgrāon Stone Inscription of the Time of Jagaddeva  
Dynasty: Paramāra
King: Jagaddeva
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose and metrical in 9 verses.
Metres: Verses 1-8, 10-11 and 13-14 Anuṣṭubh; v.9 Śārdūlavikrīḍita; v.12 Śālinī
Gist: To record the donation of a village.
Published reference: Trivedi (1978), no. 28. 


Text{1}

1. [oṃ{2}] namaḥ śivāya | kurvvanvaḥ pātu jagatāṃ prabhava sthiti saṃhri(hṛ)tīḥ | tisraḥ UˍUU--ˍU{3} viśvaṃ yatra [su]varttakaḥ |1| astyarvvu[rbbu]da
  iti khyātaḥ pratīcyāṃ diśi parvvataḥ | mekhalādyanta saṃcāri kūrmarāja divākaraḥ |2| kāmadhenu [hṛtavate]{4}
2. viśvāmitrāya kupyataḥ | vasiṣṭātatra homāgnau paramāro vyajāyata{5} |3| tad vanse(d vaṃśe) kṣatra caritaiḥ puṣpha(ṣpa)vantānvayādhike | babhūva bhoja -   
  devākhyo rājā rāma samo guṇaiḥ |4| tato riputrayaskande magnāṃ mālava[medinīṃ] | u-
3. ddharann udayādityas tasya bhrātā vyavarddhata |5| yasyācchayā diśaḥ kīrttyā bhuvanāni parairguhāḥ | [kāṣṭhāḥ] parā va(ba)lādeva, kāvyair vyāpyanta
  cārthitaḥ |6| tasya satsvapi putreṣu sva sammata sutaiṣiṇaḥ | harārādhanato jajñe jagaddevo ma[hī]-
4. patiḥ |7| divaṃ prayāte pitari svayaṃ prāptām api śriyaṃ | parivitti{6} bhayāttya[ktvā] yoggrajāya nyavedayat |8| putrāṇām prathamo'si rājya-
  viṣaya svāmī bhujo dakṣiṇaḥ sarvāsveva haristu jaṅgama [i] -
5. yat sīmā yajo mūrttimān | ātmaiveti ca saprasāda mudito yaḥ kuntala kṣmābhṛtā tādrūpyandadhadeva dakṣiṇa diśālaṅkāratāṃ puṣyati |9| arthi pratyarthino
  yasmin vā(n bā)ṇaiḥ svarṇaiśca varṣati | dainya sainya[nidhiṃ] muktvā --1{7}
6. [ṅki]tam upāsate |10| na sa deśo na sa grāmo na sa loko na sā sabhā | na tannaktaṃdivaṃ yatra jagaddevo na gīyate |11| ācandrākka
  śāsanī kṛtya tena kṣmā pālena śrī jagaddeva nāmnā | pu[ṇyāgā]raṃ{8} ḍoṅgarama nāmā grāmo dattaḥ śrīni[vā]sa dvi-
7. jāya |12| taddatta ḍoṅgaragrāme śrīnidhe[ḥ] śreyasāṃ nidheḥ | vidyāvṛtta nivāsena śrīnivāsena [sū]nuna |13| pituḥ puṇyodayāyaiḥtat kāritaṃ śiva-
  mandiram | ākalpaṃ kalpatāṃ bhūmer bhūṣaṇāya niratyayaṃ |14| atra devāya grāme[smi]n yaśca [śrīnivāsa prahi-?]
8. [vyāghātaṃ]{9} kṛtvātikṣipya{10} -- yo harttumicchati sa mahāpātakair lipyate4 | śaka saṃvat 1034 nandana saṃvatsare caitryāṃ śāsanaṃ likhitam iti |
  lekhako viśvasvā[mī] | 1034 tathāṃko 15 |

{1} From an inked impression.
{2} Expressed by a symbol which is partly visible.
{3} As shown by the traces left, the first two of the lost akṣaras may have been sthitiḥ.
{4} All these four akṣaras are lost and the reading is as suggested by Dr. V.V. Mirashi.
{5} The vertical stroke of this akṣara, as also in some instances below, is curved at its lowest extremity so
	as to give the following daṇḍa.
{6} i.e., marrying before an elder brother marries. See Manusmṛti, III, p.171, enjoining that one who does
	so is to go to hell.
{7} Read te'śaṃ, as suggested by Mirashi.
{8} Mirashi read puṇyā[dhāraṃ] and corrected it to -dhāro (see op.cit., p.184, n.2), but the consonant of the
	third of these akṣaras is clearly g with a vertical stroke ending in a triangle; cf.its form also in grāma-
	that follows.
{9} As n.7 on the preceding page.
{10} On akṣara is illegible here. The editor of Ep.Ind. remarks that "the text from atra devāya to hatturmicchati
	 seems also to be in verse." The reading of the first eight akṣaras of 108 is uncertain.
{11} For the five great sins, see Manusmṛti, XI, 54.
