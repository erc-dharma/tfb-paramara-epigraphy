Period: VS 1256 vaiśākha vadi 15
Location: Bhopal
Dynasty: Paramāra
King: Udayavarman
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Prose and Metrical in 55 verses
Metres: Verse 1-2 and 4-11 Anuṣṭubh; v.12 Indravajrā; vv.3 and Vasantatilakā; v.13 Śālinī; v.15 Puṣpitāgrā
Gist: Donation of village to a dviveda Brahmana, the Purohita Maluhuśarman, of the Gargga gotra.
Published reference: Trivedi (1978), no. 46.
