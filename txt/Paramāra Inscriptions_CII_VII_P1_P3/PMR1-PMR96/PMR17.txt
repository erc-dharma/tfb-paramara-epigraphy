PennGlobal: PMR17
Period: c. 1000-1055 AC
Location: Bhojpur 
Dynasty: Paramāra
King: Bhojadeva
Language: Sanskrit
Script: Nagari
Text: Metrical in 2 verses
Metres: Verse 1 Vasantatilaka; v.2 Upajāti
Gist: Engraved on the pedestal of a colossal image of a Jain Tīrthaṅkara in an old Jain temple.
Published reference: Trivedi (1978), no. 17. 

Text{1}

1. ------{2}[kā]re cadrārddha moli rasanaḥ sama -U-U[|*] --U-UUUUmadbhūnaki[rtti]--{3}; --U rāja parameśvara bhojadevaḥ ||1||
2. ------raḥ sāga{4}ranadi nāmā | sa nemicaṃ[dro] vidadhe pratiṣṭāṃ{5} [su]durlabhaḥ sā(śā)tijinasya [sūriḥ] ||2||

---------------------------------------------

{1} From an impression
{2} It is expected that the Siddham symbol was engraved at the beginning. Along with that, twelve syllables are lost
	here.
{3} The two akṣaras lost here may have been rāśiḥ and the following three akṣaras rājādhi- (D.C.S.).
{4} This akṣara is damaged. Fifteen syllables are lost at the beginning of this line.
{5} The slanting middle bar of the superscript of this letter is not engraved. Read -ṣṭhāṃ.
