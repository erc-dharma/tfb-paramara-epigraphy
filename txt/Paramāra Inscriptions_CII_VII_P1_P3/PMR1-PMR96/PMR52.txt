{1} From an impression.
{2} Expressed by a symbol which looks like the Nāgarī numeral 5 or 6 and is rarely found in inscriptions.
{3} It cannot be definitely known whether the figure for the tithi, which appears to have been lost, was
	incised at the end of this line or at the beginning of the next line where a small portion of the stone has
	peeled off. 
{4} This akṣara is a combination of both the palatal and dental sibilants. Sandhi is not performed
	here and the akṣara lme is ornamental, but its reading is certain. In this respect, also see Ind. Ant.,
	Vol.XVIII, p.342,n.4. Kielhorn read [ū]da[le]-. Moreover, in the end of the line there appears to be
	a sign of visarga, which may have been a scratch.
{5} The sign of the mātrā is detached from the letter. After the preceding paṃ in this line, there are traces of
	one akṣara which is lost. Probably what appears to have been intended is paṃcamahāśabdālaṃkāra.
{6} The first akṣara in this line is combined with the daṇḍa of the mātrā of the second, by a top-
	stroke.
{7} The reading of the mātrā is doubtful; it appears as that of ō also.
{8} The letters in the brackets are indistinct and are read here as required by the sense. The next line
	begins with an indistinct akṣara, which has probably scratched off, and the daṇḍas in this line, as
	some others also below, are redundant.
{9} The second letter of the name is indistinct. It appears as ru(?). For reading the name as kharehī,
	conjecturally, and its identification, see the next inscription.
{10} The two letters at the beginning of this line cannot be made out, and the reading of the last akṣara is
	uncertain.
{11} The case required here is caturthī, but the suffix is missing.
{12} The punctuation marks are redundant, as some above.
{13} Read ttaṃ. The word is written without the case ending. The preceding word is probably a short form
	of viṃśopaka or visve(?).
{14} The meaning of this word is not known to me. We have, however, pañca muṣ(kh)a pattane in the 
	 record that follows.
{15} Almost the earlier half of this line is blurred and cannot be read.
{16} This line is almost in the middle of others, leaving some space on either side. The mātrā of mā in
	mahā is struck off in the original.
