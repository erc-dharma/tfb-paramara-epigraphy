Text{1}

1. oṃ siddhiḥ || saṃvat 1312 varṣe bhādrapada su(śu di) 7 so[me]{2}
2. śrīmaddhā[rāyāṃ]{3} mahārājādhirājaśrīmajjaya[s*]i[ṃ]-
3. [ha]devavijayarājye{4} uparihāḍāmaṃ[ḍa]le{5} rājaśrī{6} . .
4. ..hāvyāpāre tasmin kāle vaṃdanā(?)dhikāre{7}
5. [tamirjo ?]ditapatrakaṃ samabhilikhyate yathā..
  ......{8}
  
----------------------------------------------------------------------

{1} From the original and impressions.
{2} What appears as an additional mātrā on the penultimate letter in this line is only a scratch.
{3} The mātrā of the first of these akṣaras is broken, leaving traces thereof, and of the second, the consonant
	is detached from the rest of the limbs. Cunningham read the first five of the akṣaras as tērasadhyaṃ,
	which gives no sense.
{4} This letter has only traces left. The fourth akṣara in this line is mutilated, but the reading is certain.
{5} The reading of the third akṣara of the name of the maṇḍala is highly doubtful, as also noted by
	Kielhorn, but here I follow him.
{6} Two akṣaras are lost here and an equal number of them at the commencement of the next line.
{7} Cunningham read tasminukālē and look Ukālēvandana as a name; but in that case, the consonant n would
	have been doubled. To me, however, this letter also appears as marked by a stroke resembling the sign
	of the mātrā of u.
{8} The rest of the inscription cannot be satisfactorily made out for reasons stated above in the article.
